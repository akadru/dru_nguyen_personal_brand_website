# Dru: Team & Content Workflow

## Current Human Process

- Two team members currently researching AI trends and leadership topics
- Monday is the key day: Dru provides ideas and a brief summary of his thoughts
- Writers take those ideas and repurpose into long-form and short-form content
- Dru reviews and edits, approves, sends back
- Team finds key visuals, sends again for review
- Dru approves, content goes live to personal brand webpage and social channels

## Source Material

- Dru has an existing persona document: who he is, his key personal stories, written with Claude
- This feeds into the content so writers can capture his voice
- Goal: build himself as a thought leader in leadership with AI

## AI Workflow to Build

### Content Infrastructure (one-time setup)
- Set up source material folder where Dru's thoughts and stories live
- Set up a content schedule/calendar with topics and dates
- Make sure folder structure is correctly arranged for agents to read

### Research Agent
- Continuously monitors AI trends, leadership with AI, and adjacent topics
- Surfaces the top 3 to 5 topic ideas that are timely and relevant to Dru's thought leadership positioning
- Presents options to Dru so he can choose what resonates with his personal stories
- Inserts approved topics into the content schedule/calendar
- This feeds the EA agent and writer with a pipeline of relevant topics

### EA / Project Manager Agent
- Knows the content schedule
- Prompts Dru every Monday: "Today is your content day, drop your thoughts here"
- Dru opens his content studio, shares ideas, and the agent drops them into source material

### Writer Agent
- Reads the content schedule to know what topic is due
- Pulls from source material (Dru's thoughts, persona, stories) and research agent output
- Drafts long-form and short-form versions of the content
- Sends back to Dru for review and approval

### Design Agent
- Once content is approved, sources or generates key visuals to accompany the piece
- Presents visual options to Dru for review and approval
- Formats content and visuals for each channel (webpage, LinkedIn, Instagram, etc.)

### Publishing Agent
- After Dru approves the content and visuals, publishes to his personal brand webpage
- Distributes to the appropriate social channels
- Follows channel-specific formatting and scheduling best practices

## Full Workflow Sequence

1. **Research Agent** surfaces trending topics relevant to Dru's positioning
2. **Dru** picks a topic and the Research Agent adds it to the content calendar
3. **EA Agent** prompts Dru on Monday to share his thoughts on the scheduled topic
4. **Dru** drops his thoughts and personal stories into the content studio
5. **Writer Agent** drafts long-form and short-form content from source material
6. **Dru** reviews, edits, and approves the written content
7. **Design Agent** sources visuals and formats for each channel
8. **Dru** reviews and approves visuals
9. **Publishing Agent** pushes content live to webpage and social channels

## Key Point
This is essentially the same process Dru already runs with human staff. The AI version just automates the research, scheduling, prompting, drafting, design, and publishing steps. Dru's approval stays in the loop at each key handoff.
