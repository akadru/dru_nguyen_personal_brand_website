8:42 AM. Skylight's monthly review starts in 18 minutes. I had nothing on screen. No deck. No one-pager. No agenda doc.

That meeting cost us 47 minutes. We argued about whose memory of last quarter was right.

I do not enter meetings that way anymore.

Anthropic shipped Claude Design on April 17. It builds the artifact in the time it used to take to write the agenda.

Here is the 30-minute prep I now run before every team meeting at Xood and Skylight:

1. Define the meeting in 10 words. One sentence is the brief.

2. Pick the deliverable type. Deck, 1-pager, decision brief, prototype. Skip color and font choices. Defaults are fine.

3. Feed the context. Last month's scorecard, the open issues, the budget memo. The artifact is only as good as the inputs.

4. Iterate one round. Cut filler. Tighten the headline. Replace any wrong number. One round, not seven.

5. Walk in driving the artifact. Share screen on slide 1. The team reads with you. They edit live. The meeting becomes about the artifact, not about everyone's memory.

A meeting without an artifact is a meeting where everyone defends their memory of reality. Add an artifact and the meeting becomes about correcting the artifact. That is a 10x better meeting.

Average time saved: 70 minutes per meeting. Across 6 to 8 weekly team meetings, that is the leverage Dan Martell talks about in his Buyback Loop, without needing to hire anyone to claim it.

If you run more than five meetings a week and you are not yet on Claude Design, you are paying a tax in time and team energy that you do not need to pay.

Try it once this week. Pick a meeting in the next 48 hours. Run the 5 steps. Walk in with the artifact.
