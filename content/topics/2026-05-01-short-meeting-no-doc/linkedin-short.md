If your team meets without a deck, you're paying for the world's most expensive water-cooler.

Last quarter at Skylight, we ran a monthly review without a single artifact on screen. 47 minutes. Nothing decided. Everyone defending their memory of last month's numbers.

The fix is not "have better meetings." The fix is *never enter a meeting without a viewing artifact.*

Anthropic's Claude Design shipped April 17. It builds the artifact in the time it used to take to write the agenda.

Try it once this week. Pick one meeting. Build the artifact in 30 minutes. Walk in driving slide 1.

The meeting becomes about correcting the artifact instead of debating everyone's memory. That's a 10x better meeting.

Knowledge with application is wisdom.
