## FAQ schema (3 Q&A)

**Q: What are the signs my team meetings are inefficient with AI?**
A: Three signals: (1) the "circle back with the numbers" reflex, where simple data isn't pre-loaded for the meeting; (2) the same argument repeating week over week, indicating no decision artifact; (3) the pre-meeting Slack scramble, which signals nobody used AI to produce a brief before the call.

**Q: How do I fix pre-meeting prep with AI?**
A: Assign one owner per recurring meeting. Their job is 30 minutes of AI prep before the call: pull the inputs, run a single brief-the-room prompt, walk in with a deck or 1-pager. Anthropic's Claude Design (launched April 2026) is purpose-built for this.

**Q: How much time do these meeting failures cost?**
A: For two businesses and advisory work, around 22 hours a week of senior-team time goes into meetings. If even 30% is wasted by these three signals, that's roughly seven hours of senior leverage per week.
