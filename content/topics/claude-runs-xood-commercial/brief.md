# Brief — How I use Claude to run Xood's commercial team in 30 min/day

## Why this post
First Writer test post. AI Operator pillar — tactical, save-worthy, ends with a usable prompt or workflow.

## Audience
Ray (plateaued operator) — 2–7 years in, stuck at scale, watching AI hype but doesn't know what's real.

## Pillar
Thinker (40%). AI Operator content. Tactical utility — Jeff Su style.

## Channel
LinkedIn long-form (Wednesday slot). 400–800 words. End with a utility takeaway, NOT a question.

## Source material
- Xood is the AI data services company Dru co-founded as MD-Commercial. Pre-revenue, Vietnam.
- Dru runs the commercial side: prospect research, outreach, partner mapping, sales-ops admin.
- Claude (Sonnet 4.6) handles ~3 hours of work daily in 30 minutes of supervision:
  1. Daily prospect digest from CRM exports
  2. First-draft outreach emails grounded in the prospect's own published material
  3. Weekly partner-map refresh with delta highlights
  4. CRM hygiene — flagging stale leads + drafting next-step actions
- The 30-min-a-day rule = morning batch.
- Mistake to avoid: treating Claude like a human assistant. Treat it like a workflow.

## Required structure (S.I.F.U for AI Operator)
- **S — Situation**: a specific morning at Xood. Real detail.
- **I — Insight**: what Dru saw that other operators miss about AI in commercial ops.
- **F — Framework**: the 4-step morning batch with one usable prompt template.
- **U — Utility takeaway**: a copy-pasteable prompt and a 10-min next action the reader can take today.
