# Audience Personas — Dru Personal Brand

Two primary personas, derived from Joy's Q2 strategy and the Content Engine SOP. Every post should be writeable to one of these (or both).

---

## Persona 1 — The Plateaued Operator ("Ray")

**Snapshot:** 2–7 years into running a business. Stuck at scale. Has the basics; needs the next level.

**Demographics**
- 30–45 years old, founder or senior operator
- Vietnam, SEA, or international with SEA ties
- Likely EO member or similar peer group
- Running a $1–10M revenue business OR pre-revenue but post-PMF

**Goals**
- Scale operations without burning out the team
- Adopt AI in a way that produces real outcomes — not theatre
- Get unstuck from the "founder bottleneck" trap
- Build durable systems (EOS, accountability charts, scorecards)

**Pains**
- Drowning in operations; everything routes through them
- Sees AI hype everywhere but can't tell what's real
- Has read the books (Traction, Rocket Fuel) but struggles with implementation
- Tired of LinkedIn influencers who've never run a P&L

**What Ray wants from Dru's content**
- Specific prompts and workflows that work in real ops (not toy demos)
- Honest stories about hard calls — what worked, what didn't, what it cost
- Frameworks that survive contact with messy reality (S.I.F.P, EOS applied to pre-revenue, Four-Layer Communication)
- Permission to say "I don't have it figured out either"

**Channel preferences**
- LinkedIn long-form (Wed) and carousel
- Substack essays
- Short-form video (saves for later)

**Voice cues that resonate**
- Real business names: Xood, Skylight, Tictag
- Operator-to-operator tone — "at an EO meeting, not a LinkedIn influencer"
- "Knowledge with application is wisdom"
- Ends with utility takeaway he can use in the next 10 minutes

---

## Persona 2 — The Curious Builder ("Linh")

**Snapshot:** First-time founder or early operator in Vietnam. Hungry. Watching how the next tier moves.

**Demographics**
- 25–35 years old, first business or first leadership role
- Based in Vietnam (Ho Chi Minh City, Hanoi, Da Nang, Nha Trang)
- May follow Dru on Facebook (Vietnamese) and LinkedIn (English)
- Building or about to build their first real venture

**Goals**
- Learn how a successful Vietnam-based operator actually thinks
- Adopt AI tooling early — get an unfair advantage
- See models for hard conversations, hiring, scaling, family dynamics
- Belong to the operator economy without coastal-elite vibes

**Pains**
- Most "founder content" is from US/EU contexts that don't map to Vietnam
- Feels alone — doesn't have an EO peer group yet
- Doesn't know who to trust on AI; doesn't want to sound like a try-hard
- Worries about disappointing family / leaving "safe" path

**What Linh wants from Dru's content**
- Stories from Vietnam-based operators (Skylight, Xood, Nha Trang)
- Vulnerable moments — quitting the family factory, the Muay Thai fight, moving back to VN
- Practical AI usage (not abstract trend pieces)
- Permission to take the unconventional path

**Channel preferences**
- Facebook Vietnamese (community + photo posts)
- LinkedIn short hooks (Sat — Human pillar)
- Substack essays for depth

**Voice cues that resonate**
- "Life happens for you, not to you."
- Signature stories: The Factory, The Move to Vietnam, The Fight
- Family + business intersected (dad reconnection, brother TK, EOS)
- Gary Vee 5% — unfiltered, willing to swear lightly when it serves

---

## Cross-cutting notes

- **Never** write to "everyone." Pick Ray, Linh, or both — and name the specific situation.
- Posts in the **Thinker** pillar typically lead with Ray; **Human** pillar typically with Linh; **Builder** crosses both.
- Vietnamese-language Facebook posts target Linh primarily. Joy / Chi write natively in Vietnamese — never translated from English.
