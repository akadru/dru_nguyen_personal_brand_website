DRU NGUYEN
Web Style Guide
Design system for consistent web pages, landing pages, and digital content across the Scholarly Warrior brand
SECTION 1
Color Palette
The Dru Nguyen web palette is built around energy, clarity, and authority. French Lime is the hero color - use it everywhere. Every color has a specific job. Do not introduce new colors.
French Lime
#ADFB49
CTA buttons on dark backgrounds, accent bars, active states, key highlights. Use everywhere unapologetically.
Dark Jungle Green
#0A1B24
Hero sections, CTA blocks, nav bar on scroll, footer backgrounds, card overlays.
Myrtle Green
#347474
Section labels, subheading accents, link colors, card border accents.
Tea Green
#D3EDC5
Card backgrounds on white pages, quote blocks, light section fills.
Pastel Blue
#B0BEC5
Secondary body text, metadata, timestamps, supporting captions.
Anti-Flash White
#F2F2F2
Default page background, light card surfaces, content section backgrounds.
Color Usage Rules
French Lime on dark backgrounds only. On white or light backgrounds it lacks contrast. Always pair with Dark Jungle Green.
Dark Jungle Green for all full-width dark sections, hero backgrounds, and CTA blocks.
Myrtle Green for section labels, links, and card accent bars. Never for body text.
Tea Green for light card fills and quote blocks. Not for text.
Never exceed this palette. No extra greens, grays, or blues outside the defined set.

SECTION 4
Component Library
Nav Bar
Sticky, top: 0. Background: rgba(10, 27, 36, 0.94) with backdrop blur.
Bottom border: 1px Pastel Blue. Height: 56px.
Logo left (Outfit Bold 16px, Anti-Flash White). CTA button right (hidden on mobile).
Hero Section
Section label in Myrtle Green above H1.
H1 in Anti-Flash White on Dark Jungle Green background.
Supporting paragraph in Pastel Blue, 22px.
Two CTAs: Primary = French Lime background, Dark Jungle Green text. Secondary = transparent with Pastel Blue border.
Section Labels
Uppercase, Outfit Bold 10px, letter-spacing 0.12em, Myrtle Green.
Always appears directly above the section heading. Example: THE PROBLEM, THE SOLUTION, HOW IT WORKS.
CTA Block
Full-width Dark Jungle Green background.
Centered headline: Outfit Bold 28px, Anti-Flash White.
Supporting text: Outfit Regular 15px, 60% white opacity.

Secondary button: transparent, 20% white border.
Quote Block
Background: Tea Green. Left border: 3px solid Myrtle Green.
Padding: 24px 28px. Text: Outfit Medium italic, 16px, Dark Jungle Green.
Card Components
Feature cards: Tea Green background, Myrtle Green 2px top accent bar.
Dark cards: Dark Jungle Green background, French Lime accent bar, Anti-Flash White text.
Team bio cards: 2px Myrtle Green top border, Myrtle Green category label, Outfit Bold 18px name.

SECTION 6
Animation
All sections use a scroll-triggered reveal animation. Elements fade in and move up 20px as they enter the viewport.
Duration
0.6s
Easing
cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)
Threshold
10% visible to trigger
Stagger
0.08s delay per element in a grid (0s, 0.08s, 0.16s for three-column)
Mobile
Same timing. Single column creates natural cascade.
Persistence
Once triggered, element stays visible permanently.
SECTION 7
Rules
Do
Use Outfit for everything. No other typefaces.
Use normal caps for all headings. Never all-caps for headlines.
Use French Lime on dark backgrounds only - never on white or light.
Use Myrtle Green for section labels, links, and card accents.
Keep the 900px max-width container on all pages.
Test every page at both desktop (900px+) and mobile (375px) widths.
Apply scroll-reveal animation to every section.
Problem first. Value second. Call to action always.
Do Not
Do not use serif fonts or any typeface other than Outfit.
Do not use French Lime on white or light backgrounds. It lacks contrast.
Do not exceed the color palette.
Do not use em dashes in any Dru Nguyen web content.
Do not break the section order. Problem before Solution.
Do not skip the scroll-reveal animation on any section.
Do not use corporate jargon or buzzwords in any copy.
Do not write passive or hedging copy. Direct and active only.
