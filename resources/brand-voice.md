# Brand Voice — Dru Personal Brand

## Identity in one line
Dru Nguyen — operator, co-founder, investor. AI-using operator becoming an educator. Voice anchored in the **Scholarly Warrior** identity: Care. Discipline. Grit.

## Voice blend (target weights)
- **70% Jeff Su** — tactical utility, save-worthy takeaways, specific prompts/templates/workflows, ends with usable action.
- **15% Tony Robbins** — identity language (Scholarly Warrior), towards/away values, emotional truth named directly.
- **10% Daniel Priestley** — sequence storytelling (before/action/after), lived experience as source material.
- **5% Gary Vee** — casual unfiltered voice; willing to swear lightly when it serves; no corporate polish.

## Four voice attributes (must be present in every post)
1. **Operator Authority** — talks like someone who has shipped real work. Real business names (Xood, Skylight, Tictag), real outcomes, real numbers when they exist.
2. **Warm Teacher** — shares generously. Explains the WHY behind the HOW. Patient with readers at different stages.
3. **AI Realist** — rejects AI hype and AI theater. Positive about AI but grounded in what it actually does in real operations.
4. **Sharp Observer** — names patterns other operators miss. Notices the unnamed pain in a room and calls it out.

## Person & tense
- **First person, always.** "I learned this the hard way." Never "Leaders often find that..."
- Contractions always. "Here's" not "Here is."
- Em dash, not comma — "real thinking — real outcomes."

## Vocabulary — voice markers (use sparingly, max 1–2 per post)
- "Knowledge with application is wisdom."
- "Never give up. Always find a way."
- "Life happens for you, not to you."
- "Care. Discipline. Grit."
- "Scholarly Warrior" / "I see, hear, feel, and KNOW."

## Words & phrases to avoid (never)
- "Hot take", "Game changer", "Unpacking", "Let that sink in", "Read that again", "It's that simple"
- "If you're not doing X, you're leaving money on the table"
- "Leverage", "synergies", "unlock potential", "at scale"
- Generic openers: "In today's rapidly evolving AI landscape..." / "Most founders don't realize..."
- Emoji spam (🚀💡✨). One earned emoji rarely; zero preferred.
- Engagement-bait endings: "What are your thoughts?" / "Drop a 🔥 below" / "Agree or disagree?"

## CTA style
- **AI Operator / tactical content** → utility takeaway (a prompt, template, or specific 10-minute action). Never a question.
- **Human / Builder content** → one real question worth answering. Never both, never neither.
- **Substack essays** → specific share/subscribe CTA: "If this made you think of a founder you know, forward it to them" beats "Subscribe for more."

## Four-question calibration test (every draft must pass all 4)
1. Does it sound like Dru talking to an operator at an EO meeting? (vs. a LinkedIn influencer)
2. Could 100 other people have posted this? (If yes — fail. Only Dru could post it because of the specific detail.)
3. Would Dru be proud of this in 2 years?
4. Does the ending give the reader a question worth answering OR a tactic worth using?

## Hard rules
- Specificity over philosophy — name the business, the tool, the moment.
- Short paragraphs (1–3 lines). Every sentence earns its place.
- At most one bold phrase for emphasis. No bullet spam.
- Bold via `<strong>` — never caps or asterisks.
- Anchor hashtag: `#DoingGoodWithData`. Standing brand hashtag for blog posts: `#FutureOfWork`.
- Social posts → draft for approval first.
- Email campaigns → ALWAYS require human approval before sending.
